Least-squares initialisation for network compression

2026

A deep teacher network is trained, then a shallower student is initialised by fitting each of its weight matrices, by least squares, to a block of the teacher's operations. On MNIST and FashionMNIST, one-hidden-layer students initialised this way beat randomly initialised ones in most configurations and often outperformed their deeper teachers; on CIFAR-10 the advantage disappeared. I also extended the method to convolutional layers by writing a convolution as a matrix product.

  • PyTorch
